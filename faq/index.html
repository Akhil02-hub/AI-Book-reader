FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What file formats are supported?

Currently, we support EPUB, PDF, plain text (.txt), and Markdown (.md) files. The application extracts the text from these formats and builds a continuous reading view.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your books, reading progress, and highlights are stored securely and are strictly private to your account. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking.

Can I listen to my books?

Yes, the built-in read-aloud feature uses your browser's native speech synthesis to narrate the text, complete with word-by-word highlighting. You can choose your system's voice and adjust the reading speed.

How do I start reading from a specific point?

Scroll to the sentence you want to start with and click "Set start here" in the reading toolbar. Alternatively, hold the Alt key and click any sentence to jump the starting position there.

Can I export my highlights?

Yes. Select any text to highlight it. When you are ready, click the Download PDF button in the top right of the reader. Your browser's print dialog will open—choose "Save as PDF" to download a copy with your highlights baked in.
