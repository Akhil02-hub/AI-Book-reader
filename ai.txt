=========================================================
PROJECT: AI BOOK READING
ROLE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
=========================================================

This project was heavily accelerated and improved with the assistance of Artificial Intelligence.

AI was used as a collaborative tool to:
- Audit security vulnerabilities (XSS prevention, rate limiting, SQL injection).
- Refactor backend architecture (database persistence, session management).
- Debug complex frontend issues (infinite loops, state management, DOM rendering).
- Optimize CSS layout and responsive design.

AI served as an accelerator, but the core product and its implementation were driven by human intent and guided by Amarachintha Akhil.
