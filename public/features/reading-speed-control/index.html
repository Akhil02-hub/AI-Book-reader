Features

Reading Speed Control.

Take control of your listening experience. Everyone processes information at a different pace, and our adjustable speed controls let you find the perfect rhythm for your brain.

Slow it down for complex text

Reading a dense academic paper or learning a new language? Slow the narration down to 0.8× to ensure you catch every single word and concept.

Speed it up for skimming

If you're listening to a familiar story or trying to power through a lot of text, you can speed up the narration to 1.2× or 1.5×. It's perfect for auditory learners who read faster than the average speaking voice.

Preferences saved

Your chosen reading speed and voice are saved in your browser, so you don't have to reconfigure them every time you start a new reading session.
