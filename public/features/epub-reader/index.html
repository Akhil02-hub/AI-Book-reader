Features

EPUB Reader.

Read your favorite EPUB books in a continuous, seamless scroll. We extract the raw text and chapters from the EPUB file and rebuild it into a clean, distraction-free reading view.

No pagination. Just reading.

Traditional e-readers force you to flip pages, which can break your focus. Our reader lays the entire book out in one continuous vertical scroll, allowing you to read naturally and stay immersed in the text.

Chapter support

We parse the EPUB index to preserve chapter breaks and titles, giving you clear visual markers as you progress through the book.

Read aloud built-in

Every EPUB uploaded can be narrated using your browser's native text-to-speech engine. You can even highlight sentences as they are spoken.
