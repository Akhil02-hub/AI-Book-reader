Features

Text to Speech.

Turn any book or document into an audiobook instantly. Our built-in text-to-speech engine reads your books aloud, allowing you to listen while you commute, exercise, or relax.

Native browser voices

We leverage the high-quality, built-in voices provided by your operating system and browser. This means no external API calls, no delays, and completely private narration.

Word-by-word highlighting

As the narrator speaks, the app automatically highlights the current sentence and the exact word being read. This visual tracking helps you stay focused and is especially useful for speed reading or learning.

Pause and resume

Need a break? Pause the narration and the app will remember exactly where you left off. When you're ready, hit resume and pick up right from the middle of the sentence.
