Privacy

Privacy Policy.

Your privacy is important. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use AI Book Reading.

What we collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account Data: Your username and a securely hashed password.
  • Book Data: The text of the books you upload (EPUB, PDF, TXT, MD) and the extracted chapters.
  • Reading Data: Your reading progress (segment position) and any highlights you create.

How we store data

Your data is stored securely on our servers using a SQLite database. We do not use third-party analytics, tracking scripts, advertising networks, or external cloud storage providers.

Highlights and Progress

Reading progress and highlights are saved to your account so you can resume where you left off across devices. When you delete a book, its associated reading data is removed.

Cookies

We use a single essential session cookie to keep you logged in. This cookie is strictly necessary for the application to function and is not used for tracking.
