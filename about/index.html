About

A quieter way to read.

AI Book Reading (Margin) is a private, continuous-scroll reading application built to help you focus on one book at a time. We strip away the noise of traditional e-readers and provide a clean, minimal interface that lets you get lost in the text.

Built by a human.

Made by Amarachintha Akhil, this project was created with a vision for a distraction-free, continuous reading experience. It leverages modern web technologies to render documents smoothly while keeping your data private and secure.

One book at a time.

Unlike massive digital libraries that overwhelm you with choices, this space is designed for intentional reading. Upload your current book, set your reading position, and let the continuous scroll guide you from the first line to the last.
